# Teamleader

> Teamleader develops business management software for SMEs, entrepreneurs, and agencies in Europe. Four product lines: **Teamleader Focus** (all-in-one business software for small businesses, combining CRM, quotations, invoicing, time tracking, project management, and work orders), **Teamleader Orbit** (premium agency software combining project management, capacity planning, timesheets and financial reporting), **Teamleader One** (Peppol e-invoicing software for the self-employed), and **Dexxter by Teamleader** (automated bookkeeping for Belgian sole proprietors). Companion product: **Vectera by Teamleader** (customer meeting and scheduling platform). Founded in 2012 in Ghent, Belgium, with an office in Amsterdam and a dedicated sales presence in Germany. Over 34,000 customers across Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, France, Spain, and Italy. Part of Visma since 2022.

## Products

- [Teamleader Focus](https://www.teamleader.eu/focus): All-in-one business management software for SMEs and entrepreneurs. CRM, quotations, invoicing, time tracking, work orders, project management. Sold across BE, NL, FR, ES, IT. 14-day free trial.
- [Teamleader Orbit](https://www.teamleader.eu/orbit): Premium work management software for agencies. Advanced project and resource planning, financial reporting, and profitability analysis per person, project, department, and client. Supports retainers, incoming invoices, freelance hours. Same go-to-market across all geographies.
- [Teamleader One](https://one.teamleader.eu/): E-invoicing software for the self-employed (eenmanszaak), part-time entrepreneurs, and small private limited companies (BV). Unlimited Peppol invoicing, accountant sharing, quotations, contacts, and dashboard. 14-day free trial. Currently sold in Belgium; launching in the Netherlands to support the EU e-invoicing mandate.
- [Dexxter by Teamleader](https://www.dexxter.be/): Automated single-entry bookkeeping software for Belgian sole proprietors, starters, and student entrepreneurs who manage their own books without a bookkeeper. Belgium only.
- [Vectera by Teamleader](https://www.vectera.com/): Customer meeting and scheduling platform. Companion product line offering video meetings and appointment booking that integrates across the Teamleader portfolio.
